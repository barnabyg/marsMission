# Art sources

Every art file in this folder, with its source and licence, as [ADR 0005](../../docs/adr/0005-free-open-licensed-media-only.md) requires.

## earth.jpg

- What: Blue Marble Next Generation, December, with topography and bathymetry. 5400×2700, simple cylindrical. Unmodified.
- Source: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography-bathymetry
- File: https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/73000/73909/world.topo.bathy.200412.3x5400x2700.jpg
- Credit: NASA Earth Observatory, Reto Stöckli (NASA Goddard Space Flight Center)
- Licence: Public domain, as a work of the US Government. See NASA's media usage guidelines: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

## mars.jpg

- What: Mars Viking Global Color Mosaic, built from NASA Viking Orbiter images. 1024×512 sample of the 925 m/pixel mosaic, simple cylindrical. Unmodified.
- Source: https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_viking_global_color_mosaic_925m
- File: https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/ckan/dataset/dfdc2242-52dc-4126-bc89-03af8253ae79/resource/0d7b31dc-0b2e-4ca6-89dc-e3c1404c0232/download/mars_viking_clrmosaic_global_1024.jpg
- Credit: NASA / USGS Astrogeology Science Center
- Licence: Public domain. The source page states "Access constraints: public domain" and "Use constraints: None".

## The Asset pictures in assets/

- What: `assets/crew.png`, `assets/fuel-plant.png`, `assets/habitat.png`, `assets/lab.png`, `assets/lander.png`, `assets/orbiter.png` and `assets/rover.png`, each a 256×256 picture of the 3D model Mission Mode draws for that kind of Asset, and for the Crewed Mission's lander, on a transparent background, shown on the Asset cards.
- Source: generated by `node scripts/generate-asset-art.mjs` from the game's own models in `src/mission`, built from three.js shapes in this repository, with no third-party material.
- Licence: CC0-1.0 (public domain dedication)
