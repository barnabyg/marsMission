# Mars Mission

A planning and simulation game in which the player runs a Mars exploration Program on a budget. Over a series of Launch Windows the player balances safety, duration, science and public support, starting with robotic precursors and working towards crewed Missions.

## Program structure

**Program**:
One complete game: a sequence of Missions flown over several Launch Windows on a single budget.
_Avoid_: Campaign, run, playthrough

**Mission**:
A single endeavour launched in one Launch Window with a defined goal, such as putting an orbiter at Mars or returning samples.
_Avoid_: Expedition, flight

**Launch Window**:
The recurring period, roughly every 26 months, when an Earth–Mars transfer is practical. Missions can only depart during one.
_Avoid_: Launch opportunity, turn

**Robotic Mission**:
A Mission with no crew aboard. It is cheaper than a Crewed Mission and risks only hardware and money.
_Avoid_: Unmanned mission, probe mission

**Crewed Mission**:
A Mission carrying astronauts. It costs much more than a Robotic Mission and earns more Public Support, and failure can cost lives.
_Avoid_: Manned mission, human mission

**Precursor**:
A Robotic Mission whose main value is lowering the risk or cost of later Missions, for example by surveying landing sites.

## Modes

**Planning Mode**:
The mode in which the player chooses Missions, technology and Safety Margin, and spends budget.
_Avoid_: Build mode, design mode

**Mission Mode**:
The graphical mode in which planned Missions play out over time and Mission Events are resolved.
_Avoid_: Flight mode, simulation mode, play mode

**Decision Point**:
A moment in Mission Mode when play pauses for the player to make a limited choice, such as aborting or using a contingency.
_Avoid_: Prompt, intervention

## Risk

**Mission Event**:
A discrete, riskable step within a Mission, such as launch, Mars transfer burn, aerocapture, entry/descent/landing or rendezvous. Each one has a failure probability.
_Avoid_: Step, phase, milestone

**Safety Margin**:
Investment the player makes to reduce risk: testing, safety equipment (such as radiation shielding) and mission options (such as an escape module). It costs money and sometimes mass.
_Avoid_: Safety budget, safety level

## Balance elements

**Science**:
The Program's score, earned by achieving science goals. It is weighted heavily towards activity at Mars itself; returning samples is worth far more than an orbital survey.
_Avoid_: Points, research

**Public Support**:
A meter of public and political backing that turns into funding between Launch Windows. If it falls to zero, the Program is cancelled.
_Avoid_: Popularity, approval, morale

**Duration**:
How long a Mission lasts. Longer Missions use more supplies, expose crew to more radiation and slowly drain Public Support.
_Avoid_: Mission length, time budget

## Technology

**Technology Readiness**:
How mature a technology is, from flight-proven to not yet built. Less mature technology costs more, needs development time before it can fly and fails more often until it has flown successfully.
_Avoid_: TRL (in player-facing text), tech level
